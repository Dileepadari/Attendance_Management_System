Before you edit by hand

  • Row 1 holds college IDs and row 2 holds names. Every row below is one class session.
  • Column S. No holds the session date, column period the period. Both must be filled in or the row is skipped when reports are built.
  • Use 1 for present and 0 for absent. A blank cell means "not enrolled for this session" and is left out of the percentage entirely.
  • Adding a student means adding a column to every subject tab, not just one.
